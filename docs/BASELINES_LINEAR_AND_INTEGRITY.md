## 1. Linear Diagnostic Baseline

Identification → Examination → Hypothesis/Assessment → Cross-examination → Conclusion/Integration

Keeps process claims objective. Prevents premature expansion of incomplete or volatile inquiry. Complementary to the Spiral of Information.

**Applicability check required first** against TCRF, association, examination, and head-to-head.

## 2. Core Integrity Principle (the knot)

Critique and generation stay inside substances and designated incomplete information already present. A claimed fact is shown untenable only by reference to what is already known and working.

Refuse both: generosity that completes the other side’s argument, and imposition of a volatile or inconsequential standard of “working fact.”

The knot is tied so well that cutting it is a crime and does not win the kingdom.

**Applicability check required first** against residual-stability claims, process differentials, and parallel product comparison.
